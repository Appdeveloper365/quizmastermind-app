Quiz Mastermind

Next-generation, local-first AI trivia learning engine running directly on your device.

Instant play — zero accounts, zero trackers: Launch Quiz Mastermind

What is Quiz Mastermind?

Type any subject — “Quantum Computing”, “Ancient Mesopotamian Law”, “Python Async/Await Patterns”, or “1990s World Cinema” — select your difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), and press Start. The AI writes a brand-new multiple-choice question on demand: four plausible options, exactly one correct answer, and an in-depth explanation.

Key Architectural Features

1. Infinite Fresh Questions & Quality Gates

Nothing is pulled from a static database. Every question is dynamically crafted, while your previous questions (up to 500 per topic) are remembered in local IndexedDB to guarantee you never encounter repetitive questions.

2. "User-Pays" AI Authentication (OAuth PKCE)

Connect your personal AI accounts with zero API markup:

  • ⚡ OpenRouter (OAuth PKCE): Direct browser authorization with no API key copy-pasting. Access Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, and Llama 3.3.
  • 🤗 Hugging Face (OAuth PKCE): Connect via official Hugging Face OAuth for Qwen 2.5 72B, DeepSeek, and open-weight models.
  • ☁️ Puter.js: Free user-pays cloud AI via your Puter account.
  • 🔑 BYOK Remote Keys: Google Gemini, Groq, Pollinations, and xAI.
  • 🌐 In-Browser AI (WebLLM): Runs models directly in your browser using WebGPU for 100% offline generation with zero API keys or accounts.

3. Privacy-First Local Storage

There are no central databases, user tracking, or advertising scripts. Your scores, streak stats, and encrypted credentials stay strictly on your device.

4. Cross-Platform Everywhere

Available as a web Progressive Web App (PWA), lightweight desktop binary (Tauri v2), and Android APK package.
