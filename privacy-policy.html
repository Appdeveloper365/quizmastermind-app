Privacy Policy

Application: Quiz Mastermind | Data Controller: Appdeveloper365 | Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Privacy by Architecture: Quiz Mastermind operates with a zero-backend, local-first model. We do not have user accounts, central databases, or telemetry tracking servers. Your game history, settings, and encrypted API credentials remain exclusively on your own device. When you generate a question, your device communicates directly with your chosen third-party AI provider via encrypted HTTPS.

1. What We Do Not Collect

Appdeveloper365 does not collect, harvest, view, or sell:

  • Personal account identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords);
  • Your quiz prompts, questions, answers, accuracy scores, or play history;
  • Behavioral telemetry, session replays, or advertising tracking cookies;
  • Financial or payment details.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All application state is stored locally on your device and can be permanently purged by you at any time:

Location Key / Identifier Purpose & Encryption
localStorage quiz.key.* API keys encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM (non-extractable WebCrypto key). If session mode is selected, keys exist in RAM only.
localStorage ai_auth_* OAuth PKCE scoped access tokens for OpenRouter or Hugging Face.
localStorage quiz.stats, quiz.recent Local score counters, streaks, and previous 8 answered questions for rapid UI rendering.
IndexedDB quiz-mastermind Recent question history pool (capped at 500 per topic) to prevent repeated questions.
Cache Storage sw.js cache / WebLLM Service worker offline app files and downloaded WebLLM model weights.

3. Direct Third-Party Data Transmission & Sub-Processors

When generating questions, network requests travel directly from your browser/device to the selected provider:

  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai): Receives topic prompts and system instructions when using OpenRouter. Governed by OpenRouter Privacy Policy.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co / router.huggingface.co): Receives inference prompts under your connected Hugging Face OAuth token. Governed by Hugging Face Privacy Policy.
  • Other Providers: Google Gemini, Groq, Pollinations, xAI, and Puter receive queries only when explicitly chosen.
  • In-Browser WebLLM: Runs models entirely inside your browser via WebGPU. Zero data leaves your machine.
  • Hosting (GitHub Pages): Static bundles are served by GitHub Pages, which logs standard connection metadata under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Desktop Loopback OAuth Disclosure

In the desktop application (Tauri v2), clicking "Continue with OpenRouter" or "Continue with Hugging Face" temporarily binds a local listener on 127.0.0.1:43821 to receive the redirect code from your browser. It serves a styled confirmation page, delivers the code to the app webview, and immediately shuts down. No data is sent to Appdeveloper365.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Because all data lives on your device and no central servers exist, you hold absolute sovereignty over your data:

  • Right to Access: View all stored keys and question histories directly inside the application and browser Developer Tools.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently delete all local data using the in-app controls ("Wipe all saved keys", "Forget what I've seen", "Clear list") or your browser's "Clear Site Data" option.
  • No Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
